# Ayunova
Mediex streamlines healthcare management by connecting patients with doctors, hospitals, and essential health resources. Whether booking appointments, analyzing medical reports, or accessing health guidance, Mediex makes it simple and accessible.

✨ Features

Patient Management

Secure patient registration and login
Comprehensive health profile management
Emergency contact information storage
Doctor Portal

Doctor dashboard with practice management
Demo doctor access for testing
Patient appointment management
Healthcare Discovery

Hospital and specialist search
Advanced filtering and discovery tools
Hospital details and location information
Appointment System

Intuitive appointment booking interface
Doctor availability management
Appointment history and tracking
Medical Reports

Medical report upload and analysis
Report history and organization
Secure document management
Health Assistant

AI-powered health information guidance
General wellness recommendations
24/7 health support
User Experience

Fully responsive design (mobile, tablet, desktop)
Modern, intuitive interface
Fast and smooth interactions
